Hi, I'm Oliver. I'm interested in AI in the broad sense of Russell and Norvig's
[_Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach_](https://aima.cs.berkeley.edu/), especially causality, reinforcement
learning, and computer vision.

I'm doing an M.S. in Artificial Intelligence at [Oregon State University](https://oregonstate.edu/), after B.S. degrees
in Mathematics and Computer Science there. I'm also a graduate research assistant in the
[Causal Intelligence and Reasoning Lab](https://karthikamohan.com/lab), working on methods that learn and use
cause-and-effect structure in data. This past summer I was on the computer vision team at
[Walmart Global Tech](https://tech.walmart.com/), moving inference off GPU servers and onto accelerators built into the
cameras.

Outside of research, I compete in [ICPC](https://icpc.global/) and the [Putnam](https://maa.org/putnam/), and I've
been vice president of OSU's [ACM chapter](https://acmosu.org/) and
[Chess Club](https://www.instagram.com/osuchess/).

Feel free to reach out by [email](mailto:oliveroldrich@gmail.com)! You can also find me on
[GitHub](https://github.com/oliverflood) and [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/oliverflood/).
