Hi, I'm Oliver. I'm interested in AI in the broad sense of Russell and Norvig's _Artificial Intelligence: A Modern
Approach_, especially causality, reinforcement learning, and computer vision.

I'm doing an M.S. in Artificial Intelligence at Oregon State University, after B.S. degrees in Mathematics and Computer
Science there. I'm also a graduate research assistant in the Causal Intelligence and Reasoning Lab, working on methods
that learn and use cause-and-effect structure in data. This past summer I was on the computer vision team at Walmart
Global Tech, moving inference off GPU servers and onto accelerators built into the cameras.

Outside of research, I compete in ICPC and the Putnam, and I've been vice president of OSU's
[ACM chapter](https://acm.oregonstate.edu) and Chess Club.

Feel free to reach out by [email](mailto:oliveroldrich@gmail.com)! You can also find me on
[GitHub](https://github.com/oliverflood) and [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/oliverflood/).
